Building permits in Columbus

Residential building permits are issued by City of Columbus Department of Building and Zoning Services. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

Columbus climate at a glance

Columbus averages 5,233 heating degree days and 1,068 cooling degree days a year, with 41.6 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.

Source: NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals 1991-2020, station USW00014821. Checked November 15, 2026.
