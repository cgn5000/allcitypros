Frequently Asked Questions

How much do solar panels cost in Buffalo?
A typical residential solar installation in Buffalo costs $15,000–$25,000 before incentives. After the 30% federal tax credit (ITC), the net cost drops to $10,500–$17,500. Most Buffalo homeowners break even in 6–10 years and save $20,000–$40,000 over the system's lifetime.
What solar incentives are available in NY?
NY homeowners can claim the 30% federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) on the full cost of their solar system. NY may also offer additional state tax credits, net metering policies, and utility rebates. A Buffalo solar installer can identify every incentive you qualify for.
How much can solar reduce my electricity bill in Buffalo?
Most Buffalo homeowners eliminate 70–100% of their electricity bill with a properly sized solar system. Your exact savings depend on your current usage, roof size and orientation, and local utility rates. A Buffalo solar company will model your savings before you sign anything.
Do solar panels work on cloudy days?
Yes — solar panels generate electricity from daylight, not direct sun. They're less efficient on cloudy days (typically 10–25% of peak output) but still produce meaningful energy. Buffalo homeowners in cloudier climates still achieve excellent payback through net metering.
How long do solar panels last?
Quality solar panels carry 25-year performance warranties and often last 30–40 years. System output degrades slowly — about 0.5% per year. Your Buffalo solar installer will show you a production model projecting output over the full warranty period.
