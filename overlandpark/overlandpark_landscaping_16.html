How often should my lawn be mowed in Overland Park?
During peak growing season, most Overland Park lawns need mowing every 7–10 days. Cool-season grasses may slow in summer heat; warm-season grasses grow fastest in summer. Your landscaper will recommend a frequency that keeps your lawn healthy without over-cutting.
What's the best time to plant in Overland Park?
Planting timing depends on your plant type and KS's climate zone. Generally, spring (after last frost) and fall (6 weeks before first frost) are ideal for most plants. A Overland Park landscape designer can recommend plants suited to your specific soil and sun conditions.
How do I get rid of weeds without hurting my lawn?
Pre-emergent herbicides applied in early spring prevent weed seeds from germinating. Post-emergent products target existing weeds.
