Building permits in San Jose

Residential building permits are issued by City of San José Planning, Building and Code Enforcement Department, Building Division. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

San Jose climate at a glance

San Jose averages 2,184 heating degree days and 624 cooling degree days a year, with 13.5 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.

Source: NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals 1991-2020, station USW00023293. Checked November 15, 2026.
