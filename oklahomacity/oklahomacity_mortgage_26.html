Frequently Asked Questions

What credit score do I need for a mortgage in Oklahoma City?
Conventional loans typically require a 620+ credit score; FHA loans accept scores as low as 580 (with 3.5% down) or 500 (with 10% down); VA loans have no minimum score set by the VA but lenders usually require 580–620. Your Oklahoma City mortgage advisor can help you understand your options.
How much do I need for a down payment in Oklahoma City?
Conventional loans start at 3% down; FHA loans require 3.5%; VA and USDA loans are 0% down for qualifying buyers. First-time buyer programs in OK may offer down payment assistance. Your Oklahoma City mortgage advisor can identify every program you qualify for.
What's the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?
Pre-qualification is a quick estimate based on self-reported information. Pre-approval is a verified commitment — lenders check your credit, income, and assets and issue a formal letter. In competitive Oklahoma City markets, sellers strongly prefer buyers with pre-approval letters.
How long does it take to close on a home in Oklahoma City?
Most purchase loans close in 30–45 days; refinances typically close in 20–30 days. Your Oklahoma City mortgage company will give you a clear timeline at application. Delays usually stem from appraisals, title issues, or missing documentation — your loan officer will keep you informed.
Is it worth refinancing my mortgage right now?
The classic rule is to refinance if you can drop your rate by 1%+ and recoup closing costs within 2 years. But every situation is different. A Oklahoma City mortgage advisor can run a break-even analysis in minutes to show you exactly when refinancing makes sense for your loan.
