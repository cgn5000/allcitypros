Building permits in Cape Coral

Residential building permits are issued by City of Cape Coral Development Services, Permitting Services Division. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

Cape Coral climate at a glance

Cape Coral averages 252 heating degree days and 4,046 cooling degree days a year, with 57.4 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.
