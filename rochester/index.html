Building permits in Rochester

Residential building permits are issued by City of Rochester Department of Neighborhood and Business Development, Permit Office. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

Rochester climate at a glance

Rochester averages 6,273 heating degree days and 677 cooling degree days a year, with 35.1 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.
