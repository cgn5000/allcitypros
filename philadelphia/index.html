Building permits in Philadelphia

Residential building permits are issued by City of Philadelphia Department of Licenses and Inspections. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

Philadelphia climate at a glance

Philadelphia averages 4,466 heating degree days and 1,375 cooling degree days a year, with 45.0 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.
