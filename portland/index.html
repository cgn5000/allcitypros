Building permits in Portland

Residential building permits are issued by City of Portland Permitting & Development. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

Portland climate at a glance

Portland averages 4,104 heating degree days and 508 cooling degree days a year, with 36.9 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.

Source: NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals 1991-2020, station USW00024229. Checked November 15, 2026.
