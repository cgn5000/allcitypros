Before you hire in Yonkers: a checklist

  1. New York has no statewide license for this work, so ask which local license or registration the HVAC contractor holds and confirm it with the issuing city or county.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Yonkers Department of Housing and Buildings and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our HVAC guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

HVAC licensing in New York

New York State does not issue a statewide HVAC contractor license; licensing is handled by cities and counties, so homeowners should check with their local building department. In New York City, the Department of Buildings licenses oil burner equipment installers.

Building permits in Yonkers

Residential building permits for Yonkers are issued by City of Yonkers Department of Housing and Buildings. Permits are commonly required for replacing or adding heating and cooling equipment. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Yonkers climate and what it means for heating and cooling

Heating demand is far larger than cooling demand here. Yonkers has more heating degree days than 104 of the 155 cities we cover and more cooling degree days than 64. Seasonal notes for New York

Heating reliability is the priority in New York. Schedule furnace or boiler service before the first hard freeze, and ask about sizing, venting and backup options if your system fails in a cold snap.
