Plumbing licensing in Tennessee

Tennessee requires a contractor's license from the Board for Licensing Contractors for plumbing work of $25,000 or more. The board also issues a Limited Licensed Plumber (LLP) license for smaller jobs, which is accepted only in some cities and counties, so homeowners should check local rules.

Building permits in Clarksville

Residential building permits for Clarksville are issued by City of Clarksville Building & Codes Department. Permits are commonly required for water heaters, gas lines and repiping. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Tennessee

Occasional hard freezes in Tennessee can burst exposed pipes. Insulate outdoor spigots and pipes in unheated spaces and know where your main shutoff is.
