⚖️ Lawyers in New York, NY

License checks, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring a lawyer in New York? Start with the facts that are specific to New York and New York: who licenses the work, how to check a license, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in New York: a checklist

  1. Look the attorney up in the official New York license lookup and confirm the license is active and in good standing.
  2. Check the lawyer's public discipline history, ask how they bill (hourly, flat or contingency) and get the fee agreement in writing.
  3. Talk to two or three lawyers before you sign; our legal guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Checking a lawyer's license in New York

Lawyers in New York are admitted to the bar by the courts and must register with the Unified Court System every two years. The court system's online attorney directory lets the public look up an attorney's registration.

New York's legal market: firms and pay

Census County Business Patterns counted 5,252 business locations with paid employees in New York City (five boroughs) in 2023 in the industry “offices of lawyers,” employing 76,838 people. The median annual wage for lawyers in the New York-Newark-Jersey City, NY-NJ metro area was $208,880 (May 2025), higher than in most of the 83 metro areas we cover. Local pay levels feed into the hourly rates firms charge.
