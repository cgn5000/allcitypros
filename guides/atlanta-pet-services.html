🐾 Pet Services in Atlanta, GA

Costs, how to hire and what to ask · Updated October 2026

Choosing someone to care for your pet is a trust decision as much as a pricing one. This guide helps Atlanta pet owners compare groomers, boarders, walkers and trainers.

Atlanta is in Georgia, which generally has hot, humid summers and mild winters. That shapes what pet services work looks like locally and when it is best to schedule it.

What to know in Atlanta

Pet licensing, vaccination and leash rules vary by city and county. Check with Atlanta animal services for local requirements, and ask providers about vaccination requirements and emergency plans.

How to hire the right pet service provider in Atlanta

  1. Decide what service you need and how often.
  2. Ask for a meet-and-greet or facility tour before booking.
  3. Check vaccination requirements, emergency policies and staff experience.
  4. Confirm insurance and bonding for in-home services.
  5. Start with a short trial before longer stays.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • What vaccinations do you require?
  • What is your emergency and veterinary plan?
  • Are you insured and bonded?
  • How many pets are in your care at once?
  • How will you update me while I'm away?

Red flags to watch for

  • Unwillingness to show the facility.
  • No vaccination or emergency policy.
  • Cash-only with no written agreement.
  • Overcrowded or unsupervised play areas.

Licensing and verification in Georgia

Pet service rules vary by city and state; boarding and grooming facilities may require business or kennel licenses. Check with Atlanta animal services.

When to schedule

Book holiday boarding and sitting weeks or months in advance.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog boarding cost?
Boarding commonly runs from about $40 to $100 per night depending on market and services.
What should I ask a pet sitter?
Ask about experience, insurance, emergency plans and how they handle medications and updates.
Do groomers require vaccinations?
Most do. Confirm requirements before your appointment.
