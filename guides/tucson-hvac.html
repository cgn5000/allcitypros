Before you hire in Tucson: a checklist

  1. Look the HVAC contractor up in the official Arizona license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: Unlicensed handyman work is allowed only for casual or minor jobs where the total contract price, including labor, materials, and all other items, is less than $1,000, and only if no building permit is required and the job is not part of a larger project; unlicensed workers must state "not a licensed contractor" in advertising. The exemption does not cover fire safety work or connections to natural gas, propane, or other fuel supplies.
  3. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Tucson Planning & Development Services and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our HVAC guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

HVAC licensing in Arizona

Arizona licenses air conditioning and refrigeration contractors through the Registrar of Contractors (residential classification R-39, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration). A license is required unless the job falls under the state's under-$1,000 handyman exemption.

Building permits in Tucson

Residential building permits for Tucson are issued by City of Tucson Planning & Development Services. Permits are commonly required for replacing or adding heating and cooling equipment. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Tucson climate and what it means for heating and cooling

Cooling demand is far larger than heating demand here. Tucson has more heating degree days than 32 of the 155 cities we cover and more cooling degree days than 134. Degree days track how hard a system works over a year, so they are a fair guide to whether to prioritize furnace or heat-pump heating capacity, cooling capacity and dehumidification, and how quickly an efficiency upgrade pays back.

Seasonal notes for Arizona

Cooling dominates in Arizona: dust, extreme heat and long run-times shorten equipment life. Ask about filter changes, coil cleaning and whether the system is rated for high outdoor temperatures.
