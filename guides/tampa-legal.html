Before you hire in Tampa: a checklist

  1. Look the attorney up in the official Florida license lookup and confirm the license is active and in good standing.
  2. Check the lawyer's public discipline history, ask how they bill (hourly, flat or contingency) and get the fee agreement in writing.
  3. Talk to two or three lawyers before you sign; our legal guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Checking a lawyer's license in Florida

Lawyers practicing in Florida are members of The Florida Bar. Its online Lawyer Directory lets the public look up a lawyer by name and see their membership status.
