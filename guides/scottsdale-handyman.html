🛠️ Handyman in Scottsdale, AZ

Costs, how to hire and what to ask · Updated October 2026

A good handyman can tackle a long to-do list in one visit. This guide explains typical rates, what handymen can and can't do, and how to hire one in Scottsdale.

Scottsdale is in Arizona, which generally has very hot, dry summers and mild winters. That shapes what handyman work looks like locally and when it is best to schedule it.

What to know in Scottsdale

Sun and dryness crack caulk, trim and weatherstripping in Arizona. Regular exterior touch-ups prevent bigger repairs.

How to hire the right handyman in Scottsdale

  1. Make a written list of every task and group them into one visit.
  2. Ask for an hourly rate and any minimum, or a flat price for the full list.
  3. Confirm the handyman is insured and clarify what is outside their scope.
  4. Ask whether materials are included.
  5. Pay when work is completed to your satisfaction.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • Are you insured?
  • Do you charge hourly or per job, and is there a minimum?
  • Are materials included?
  • Which jobs do you not take on (electrical, plumbing, structural)?
  • Can you provide recent references?

Red flags to watch for

  • No insurance.
  • Taking on licensed-trade work that requires a license.
  • Large up-front payments.
  • Refusing a written estimate.

Licensing and verification in Arizona

Handymen are often limited to small jobs under a dollar limit set by the state. Work involving licensed trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) should go to a licensed contractor. Check Arizona rules.

When to schedule

Batch tasks into one visit and avoid peak seasons like early spring and fall when homeowners schedule repairs.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a handyman cost?
Hourly rates commonly run about $50–$130 depending on market and skill, often with a one- to two-hour minimum.
What can a handyman do?
Typically small repairs and installs: patching drywall, hanging doors, mounting TVs, minor carpentry and assembly.
When should I hire a licensed contractor instead?
For electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structural or large remodeling work, hire a licensed contractor.
