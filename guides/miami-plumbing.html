🔧 Plumbing in Miami, FL

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for plumbing work in Miami? Start with the facts that are specific to Miami and Florida: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in Miami: a checklist

  1. Look the plumber up in the official Florida license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: Licensing exemption applies only to casual, minor, or inconsequential work where the total contract price for labor, materials, and all other items is less than $2,500; it does not apply to work that is part of a larger project or to anyone who advertises as a contractor.
  3. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Miami Building Department and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our plumbing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Plumbing licensing in Florida

Florida licenses plumbing contractors through the DBPR Construction Industry Licensing Board (CF certified / RF registered). Certified contractors may work anywhere in the state; registered contractors may work only in the local jurisdictions where they are registered.

Building permits in Miami

Residential building permits for Miami are issued by City of Miami Building Department. Permits are commonly required for water heaters, gas lines and repiping. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Miami's plumbing market: firms and pay

Census County Business Patterns counted 1,046 business locations with paid employees in Miami-Dade County in 2023 in the industry “plumbing, heating and air-conditioning contractors,” employing 7,119 people. The median annual wage for plumbers, pipefitters and steamfitters in the Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL metro area was $57,760 (May 2025), lower than in most of the 108 metro areas we cover. Seasonal notes for Florida

Warm, humid conditions in Florida can speed corrosion and encourage slow leaks under sinks and around water heaters. A yearly look at supply lines and the water heater is cheap insurance.
