⚡ Electrical in Boston, MA

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for electrical work in Boston? Start with the facts that are specific to Boston and Massachusetts: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in Boston: a checklist

  1. Look the electrician up in the official Massachusetts license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Boston Inspectional Services Department and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our electrical guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Electrical licensing in Massachusetts

Massachusetts licenses electricians through the Board of State Examiners of Electricians. License status and complaint history can be checked through the Division of Occupational Licensure's Check a License database.

Building permits in Boston

Residential building permits for Boston are issued by City of Boston Inspectional Services Department. Permits are commonly required for panel upgrades, new circuits and EV chargers. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Boston's electrical market: firms and pay

Census County Business Patterns counted 112 business locations with paid employees in Suffolk County in 2023 in the industry “electrical contractors and other wiring installation contractors,” employing 1,909 people. The median annual wage for electricians in the Boston-Cambridge-Newton, MA-NH metro area was $79,910 (May 2025), higher than in most of the 108 metro areas we cover.
