Before you hire in Huntsville: a checklist

  1. Look the electrician up in the official Alabama license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Huntsville Inspection Department and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our electrical guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Building permits in Huntsville

Residential building permits for Huntsville are issued by City of Huntsville Inspection Department. Permits are commonly required for panel upgrades, new circuits and EV chargers. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
