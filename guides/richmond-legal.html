Before you hire in Richmond: a checklist

  1. Look the attorney up in the official Virginia license lookup and confirm the license is active and in good standing.
  2. Check the lawyer's public discipline history, ask how they bill (hourly, flat or contingency) and get the fee agreement in writing.
  3. Talk to two or three lawyers before you sign; our legal guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Checking a lawyer's license in Virginia

Lawyers practicing in Virginia must be members of the Virginia State Bar; active (plenary) members in good standing may practice law. The Bar's lawyer directory shows each attorney's membership status.

Richmond's legal market: firms and pay

Census County Business Patterns counted 222 business locations with paid employees in Richmond city in 2023 in the industry “offices of lawyers,” employing 3,097 people. The median annual wage for lawyers in the Richmond, VA metro area was $144,910 (May 2025), higher than in most of the 83 metro areas we cover.
