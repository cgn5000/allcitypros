🐛 Pest Control in Lubbock, TX

Costs, how to hire and what to ask · Updated October 2026

Pest problems usually grow if ignored, but overpaying for unnecessary treatments is common. This guide helps Lubbock homeowners understand typical costs and choose a reputable provider.

Lubbock is in Texas, which generally has hot, humid summers and mild winters. That shapes what pest control work looks like locally and when it is best to schedule it.

What to know in Lubbock

Warm, humid conditions in Texas favor termites, ants, roaches and mosquitoes nearly year-round. Ask about a termite inspection and a recurring plan rather than one-off sprays.

How to hire the right pest control company in Lubbock

  1. Identify the pest if you can, and photograph it or save a sample.
  2. Ask for an inspection and a written treatment plan that names the pest and treatment method.
  3. Compare one-time treatment versus a recurring plan.
  4. Check licensing and insurance, and ask which products will be used around kids and pets.
  5. Confirm re-treatment and warranty terms in writing.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • What pest is this, and how do you know?
  • Which products will you use, and are they safe for children and pets?
  • What is covered if pests return, and for how long?
  • Is the plan recurring, and can I cancel without a penalty?
  • Are your technicians licensed in Texas?

Red flags to watch for

  • Pressure to sign long contracts on the first visit.
  • No inspection before quoting.
  • Unwillingness to name the products used.
  • Guarantees that sound too good to be true.

Licensing and verification in Texas

Pest control companies and technicians are typically licensed or certified at the state level. Check with the Texas agency that regulates pesticide applicators.

When to schedule

Schedule termite inspections annually and prevention plans before peak pest seasons.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I have pest control?
Many homeowners use quarterly service for general prevention, with extra visits as needed for specific infestations.
Is pest control safe for pets?
Ask which products are used and the recommended re-entry time. Many companies offer pet-safe options.
Do I need a termite inspection?
In termite-prone areas, annual inspections are common, and lenders sometimes require them when buying a home.
