Before you hire in Madison: a checklist

  1. Look the electrician up in the official Wisconsin license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Madison Development Services Center (Building Inspection Division) and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our electrical guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Electrical licensing in Wisconsin

Wisconsin's DSPS issues Electrical Contractor credentials to businesses and licenses individual electricians (such as master and journeyman electricians). Homeowners can check a credential in DSPS's LicensE license lookup.

Building permits in Madison

Residential building permits for Madison are issued by City of Madison Development Services Center (Building Inspection Division). Permits are commonly required for panel upgrades, new circuits and EV chargers. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
