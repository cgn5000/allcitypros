Before you hire in Laredo: a checklist

  1. Texas has no statewide license for this work, so ask which local license or registration the roofing contractor holds and confirm it with the issuing city or county.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our roofing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Roofing licensing in Texas

Texas has no statewide license requirement for roofing contractors. Homeowners should check with their city or county for any local registration or permit rules.

Seasonal notes for Texas

Intense sun, heavy rain and wind-driven storms are the main stressors on Texas roofs. Ask about wind-rated shingles, proper underlayment and ventilation, and photograph storm damage before calling your insurer.
