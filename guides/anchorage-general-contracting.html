Before you hire in Anchorage: a checklist

  1. Look the general contractor up in the official Alaska license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: Handyman contractors may only bid or work on projects with a total contract cost of not more than $10,000, including labor and materials; a Residential Contractor Endorsement is required to undertake 25% or more of the value of a privately owned residential structure of one to four units.
  3. Ask for proof of the insurance or bond Alaska requires (listed below) and current workers' compensation coverage.

General Contracting licensing in Alaska

Alaska requires construction contractors to hold a state Certificate of Registration, and general contractors who build or alter 25% or more of the value of a one- to four-unit home must also hold a Residential Contractor Endorsement. Handyman registrants are limited to projects of $10,000 or less including labor and materials.

Building permits in Anchorage

Residential building permits for Anchorage are issued by Municipality of Anchorage Development Services. Permits are commonly required for remodels, additions and structural work. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
