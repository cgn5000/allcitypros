Before you hire in Newport News: a checklist

  1. Look the roofing contractor up in the official Virginia license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: A contractor license is required for a single contract or project over $1,000; Class C covers over $1,000 but less than $30,000 per project (and less than $250,000 within any 12-month period).
  3. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Newport News Codes Compliance and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our roofing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Roofing licensing in Virginia

Virginia licenses roofers as contractors under the Board for Contractors, using the Roofing Contracting (ROC) specialty on a Class A, B, or C contractor license.

Building permits in Newport News

Residential building permits for Newport News are issued by City of Newport News Codes Compliance. Permits are commonly required for re-roofing and structural roof repairs. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Virginia

Virginia roofs see hail, wind, heat and freeze-thaw cycles. After a storm, get an independent inspection before signing anything with a contractor who knocked on your door.
