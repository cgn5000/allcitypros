Before you hire in Shreveport: a checklist

  1. Look the electrician up in the official Louisiana license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: Electrical/mechanical/plumbing projects exceeding $10,000 including labor and materials (LSLBC commercial license requirement).
  3. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Shreveport Permits & Inspections and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our electrical guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Electrical licensing in Louisiana

LSLBC licenses electrical contractors; under its commercial license requirements, electrical projects over $10,000 including labor and materials require an LSLBC license.

Building permits in Shreveport

Residential building permits for Shreveport are issued by City of Shreveport Permits & Inspections. Permits are commonly required for panel upgrades, new circuits and EV chargers. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
