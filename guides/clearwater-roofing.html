Before you hire in Clearwater: a checklist

  1. Look the roofing contractor up in the official Florida license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: Licensing exemption applies only to casual, minor, or inconsequential work where the total contract price for labor, materials, and all other items is less than $2,500; it does not apply to work that is part of a larger project or to anyone who advertises as a contractor.
  3. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Clearwater Planning & Development Department and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our roofing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Roofing licensing in Florida

Florida licenses roofing contractors through the DBPR Construction Industry Licensing Board (CC certified / RC registered). Certified contractors may work anywhere in the state; registered contractors may work only in the local jurisdictions where they are registered.

Building permits in Clearwater

Residential building permits for Clearwater are issued by City of Clearwater Planning & Development Department. Permits are commonly required for re-roofing and structural roof repairs. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Florida

Intense sun, heavy rain and wind-driven storms are the main stressors on Florida roofs. Ask about wind-rated shingles, proper underlayment and ventilation, and photograph storm damage before calling your insurer.
