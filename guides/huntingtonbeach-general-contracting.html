Before you hire in Huntington Beach: a checklist

  1. Look the general contractor up in the official California license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: License required when labor, materials, and all other costs total $1,000 or more, or when the job requires a permit or the person hires workers (effective January 1, 2025).
  3. Ask for proof of the insurance or bond California requires (listed below) and current workers' compensation coverage.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Huntington Beach Community Development Department, Building & Inspections Division and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our general contracting guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

General Contracting licensing in California

California licenses general building contractors through the CSLB under the B (General Building) classification. Since January 1, 2025, a license in the appropriate classification is required for jobs of $1,000 or more, and for smaller jobs that need a building permit or use hired workers.

Building permits in Huntington Beach

Residential building permits for Huntington Beach are issued by City of Huntington Beach Community Development Department, Building & Inspections Division. Permits are commonly required for remodels, additions and structural work. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
