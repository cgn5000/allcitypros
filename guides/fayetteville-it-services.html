How to hire the right IT provider in Fayetteville

  1. List your systems, users and critical applications.
  2. Decide between break-fix support and a managed services agreement.
  3. Request proposals that define response times, coverage hours and what's excluded.
  4. Ask about backup, security, monitoring and disaster recovery.
  5. Review the contract term, exit rights and who owns administrator credentials.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • What are your response and resolution times?
  • How do you handle backups and recovery testing?
  • What security tools and practices are included?
  • Who holds admin credentials and documentation?
  • Can I see a sample report and speak to a client?

Red flags to watch for

  • No clear service-level agreement.
  • Provider holds all admin credentials.
  • Vague security answers.
  • Long auto-renewing contracts without review.

Licensing and verification in North Carolina

IT services are not generally licensed, though providers may hold vendor certifications. Ask about relevant security certifications and insurance (including cyber liability).

When to schedule

Review your IT setup before growth, relocation or compliance deadlines.

Frequently asked questions

What is managed IT?
A provider monitors and supports your systems for a recurring monthly fee, usually per user or per device.
How much does IT support cost?
Many small businesses pay roughly $100–$250 per user per month for managed services, depending on scope.
Do I need cybersecurity services?
Most businesses do. At minimum, ask about backups, multi-factor authentication, patching and employee training.
