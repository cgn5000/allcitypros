🏠 Roofing in McAllen, TX

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for roofing work in McAllen? Start with the facts that are specific to McAllen and Texas: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in McAllen: a checklist

  1. Texas has no statewide license for this work, so ask which local license or registration the roofing contractor holds and confirm it with the issuing city or county.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of McAllen Building Permits & Inspections Department and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our roofing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Roofing licensing in Texas

Texas has no statewide license requirement for roofing contractors. Homeowners should check with their city or county for any local registration or permit rules.

Building permits in McAllen

Residential building permits for McAllen are issued by City of McAllen Building Permits & Inspections Department. Permits are commonly required for re-roofing and structural roof repairs. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Texas

Intense sun, heavy rain and wind-driven storms are the main stressors on Texas roofs. Ask about wind-rated shingles, proper underlayment and ventilation, and photograph storm damage before calling your insurer.
