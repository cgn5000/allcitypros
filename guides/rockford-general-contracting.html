Before you hire in Rockford: a checklist

  1. Illinois has no statewide license for this work, so ask which local license or registration the general contractor holds and confirm it with the issuing city or county.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Rockford Construction & Development Services and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our general contracting guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

General Contracting licensing in Illinois

General contractors are not required by Illinois state law to be licensed, but municipalities may require licenses, permits or other local requirements. Homeowners should check with their city or county before hiring.

Building permits in Rockford

Residential building permits for Rockford are issued by City of Rockford Construction & Development Services. Permits are commonly required for remodels, additions and structural work. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
