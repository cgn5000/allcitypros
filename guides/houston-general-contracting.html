🔨 General Contracting in Houston, TX

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for general contracting work in Houston? Start with the facts that are specific to Houston and Texas: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in Houston: a checklist

  1. Texas has no statewide license for this work, so ask which local license or registration the general contractor holds and confirm it with the issuing city or county.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Houston Permitting Center and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our general contracting guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

General Contracting licensing in Texas

Texas has no statewide licensing requirement for general contractors, though some trades (such as electrical, air conditioning, and plumbing) need state licenses. Homeowners should check with their city or county for local registration and permit rules.

Building permits in Houston

Residential building permits for Houston are issued by City of Houston Permitting Center. Permits are commonly required for remodels, additions and structural work. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
