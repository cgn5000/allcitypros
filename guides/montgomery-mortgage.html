🏡 Mortgage in Montgomery, AL

Costs, how to hire and what to ask · Updated October 2026

A mortgage is likely the biggest financial product you'll ever buy, and small differences in rate and fees add up. This guide helps Montgomery buyers and refinancers compare lenders. It is general education, not financial advice.

Montgomery is in Alabama, which generally has hot, humid summers and mild winters. That shapes what mortgage work looks like locally and when it is best to schedule it.

What to know in Montgomery

Property taxes, insurance costs and closing costs vary across Alabama and even between counties around Montgomery. Ask each lender to estimate escrow for the specific property and compare written Loan Estimates side by side.

How to hire the right mortgage lender in Montgomery

  1. Check your credit reports and understand your budget before applying.
  2. Get Loan Estimates from at least three lenders on the same day or within a short window.
  3. Compare rate, points, lender fees and closing costs on the Loan Estimate form.
  4. Ask about loan programs (conventional, FHA, VA, USDA) and which you qualify for.
  5. Lock your rate when you're comfortable, and understand lock period and fees.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • What is your NMLS ID?
  • What is the interest rate, APR and total closing cost?
  • Are there prepayment penalties?
  • How long is the rate lock and what does extending cost?
  • Who services the loan after closing?

Red flags to watch for

  • Pressure to lock or apply immediately.
  • Refusing to provide a Loan Estimate.
  • Fees that change significantly without explanation.
  • Guaranteed approval claims.

Licensing and verification in Alabama

Mortgage loan originators must be licensed and have an NMLS ID. Verify the lender and individual originator using the NMLS Consumer Access website and check Alabama regulator records.

When to schedule

Shop within a short window so inquiries count together, and avoid opening new credit before closing.

Frequently asked questions

How many lenders should I compare?
Getting at least three Loan Estimates helps you spot differences in rates and fees.
What is PMI?
Private mortgage insurance, typically required on conventional loans with less than 20% down.
Should I pay points?
Points lower the rate in exchange for upfront cost. Compare how long it takes to break even.
