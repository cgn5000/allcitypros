Before you hire in Tempe: a checklist

  1. Look the electrician up in the official Arizona license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: Unlicensed handyman work is allowed only for casual or minor jobs where the total contract price, including labor, materials, and all other items, is less than $1,000, and only if no building permit is required and the job is not part of a larger project; unlicensed workers must state "not a licensed contractor" in advertising. The exemption does not cover fire safety work or connections to natural gas, propane, or other fuel supplies.
  3. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Tempe Community Development Department, Building Safety Division and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our electrical guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Electrical licensing in Arizona

Arizona licenses electrical contractors through the Registrar of Contractors (residential classification R-11, Electrical). A license is required unless the job falls under the state's under-$1,000 handyman exemption.

Building permits in Tempe

Residential building permits for Tempe are issued by City of Tempe Community Development Department, Building Safety Division. Permits are commonly required for panel upgrades, new circuits and EV chargers. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
