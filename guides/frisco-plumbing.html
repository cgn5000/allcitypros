Before you hire in Frisco: a checklist

  1. Look the plumber up in the official Texas license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Frisco and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our plumbing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Plumbing licensing in Texas

The Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners licenses and regulates the plumbing trade in Texas. Its public license search lets homeowners look up a plumber's license.

Building permits in Frisco

Residential building permits for Frisco are issued by City of Frisco. Permits are commonly required for water heaters, gas lines and repiping. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Texas

Warm, humid conditions in Texas can speed corrosion and encourage slow leaks under sinks and around water heaters. A yearly look at supply lines and the water heater is cheap insurance.
