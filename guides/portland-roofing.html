🏠 Roofing in Portland, OR

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for roofing work in Portland? Start with the facts that are specific to Portland and Oregon: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in Portland: a checklist

  1. Look the roofing contractor up in the official Oregon license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for proof of the insurance or bond Oregon requires (listed below) and current workers' compensation coverage.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Portland Permitting & Development and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our roofing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Roofing licensing in Oregon

Oregon requires anyone who works for compensation in construction activity involving improvements to real property to hold a Construction Contractors Board license, and the CCB lists roofing as work that requires one.

Building permits in Portland

Residential building permits for Portland are issued by City of Portland Permitting & Development. Permits are commonly required for re-roofing and structural roof repairs. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Oregon

Rain, moss and algae are common roof issues in parts of Oregon; inland areas add heat stress. Ask about moss-resistant materials and proper flashing and drainage.
