Before you hire in New Orleans: a checklist

  1. Look the attorney up in the official Louisiana license lookup and confirm the license is active and in good standing.
  2. Check the lawyer's public discipline history, ask how they bill (hourly, flat or contingency) and get the fee agreement in writing.
  3. Talk to two or three lawyers before you sign; our legal guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

New Orleans's legal market: firms and pay

Census County Business Patterns counted 652 business locations with paid employees in Orleans Parish in 2023 in the industry “offices of lawyers,” employing 5,572 people. The median annual wage for lawyers in the New Orleans-Metairie, LA metro area was $121,290 (May 2025), lower than in most of the 83 metro areas we cover.
