🧹 Residential Cleaning in Virginia Beach, VA

Costs, how to hire and what to ask · Updated October 2026

Cleaning services range from solo cleaners to large franchises. This guide helps Virginia Beach households compare options, understand pricing and set clear expectations.

Virginia Beach is in Virginia, which generally has four distinct seasons with hot, humid summers and cool winters. That shapes what residential cleaning work looks like locally and when it is best to schedule it.

What to know in Virginia Beach

Ask any Virginia Beach cleaning company whether its cleaners are employees or contractors, what insurance it carries and what is included in a standard visit versus a deep clean. Scope differences explain most price differences.

How to hire the right cleaning service in Virginia Beach

  1. Decide whether you need recurring service, a one-time deep clean or a move-in/out clean.
  2. Ask for a written checklist so you know what's included.
  3. Compare pricing by flat rate versus hourly, and clarify supplies.
  4. Ask about insurance, background checks and whether cleaners are employees.
  5. Start with a trial visit and give feedback quickly.

Questions to ask before you hire

  • Are cleaners employees or contractors, and are they insured and bonded?
  • What is included in a standard visit?
  • Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
  • What is your cancellation and satisfaction policy?
  • How do you handle breakage or damage?

Red flags to watch for

  • No insurance or background checks.
  • Vague checklists that cause disputes.
  • Large up-front payments for recurring service.
  • Pressure to sign long contracts.

Licensing and verification in Virginia

Cleaning services typically need a general business license rather than a trade license, but rules vary by city. Check requirements with Virginia Beach and confirm the company carries liability insurance.

When to schedule

Book recurring slots early for popular days, and schedule move-out cleans for when the property is empty.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a house cleaning cost?
Standard cleans for an average home often fall in the $120–$300 range, with deep cleans costing more. Size, condition and market affect price.
Do I need to be home?
Not necessarily. Many households give access by key or code once they trust the provider.
What's the difference between standard and deep cleaning?
A standard clean covers regular surface cleaning; a deep clean adds detailed work like baseboards, inside appliances and built-up grime.
