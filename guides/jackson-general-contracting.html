Before you hire in Jackson: a checklist

  1. Look the general contractor up in the official Mississippi license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Know the threshold: residential building over $50,000; residential remodeling over $10,000; commercial work over $50,000.
  3. Ask for proof of the insurance or bond Mississippi requires (listed below) and current workers' compensation coverage.
  4. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Jackson Planning and Development Department and that final inspection is included in the price.
  5. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our general contracting guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

General Contracting licensing in Mississippi

Mississippi requires a Residential Builder license for new residential construction over $50,000 and a Residential Remodeler license for remodeling or additions over $10,000. Commercial jobs over $50,000 require a commercial contractor license.

Who licenses itMississippi State Board of Contractors (MSBOC)
When a license is requiredresidential building over $50,000; residential remodeling over $10,000; commercial work over $50,000
Insurance / bondApplicants must provide proof of general liability insurance; residential licenses have no minimum coverage, while commercial applicants must show at least $300,000 per occurrence and $600,000 aggregate.

Building permits in Jackson

Residential building permits for Jackson are issued by City of Jackson Planning and Development Department. Permits are commonly required for remodels, additions and structural work. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.
