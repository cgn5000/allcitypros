Before you hire in Omaha: a checklist

  1. Look the roofing contractor up in the official Nebraska license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.

Roofing licensing in Nebraska

Nebraska has no roofing-specific license; roofers, like all contractors and subcontractors doing work on others' property in Nebraska, must register with the Nebraska Department of Labor. Registration does not guarantee quality of work.

Who licenses itNebraska Department of Labor (Contractor Registration)

Seasonal notes for Nebraska

Snow load, ice dams and freeze-thaw damage drive roofing work in Nebraska. Attic insulation and ventilation are as important as the shingles themselves.
