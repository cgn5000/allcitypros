🏠 Roofing in Raleigh, NC

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for roofing work in Raleigh? Start with the facts that are specific to Raleigh and North Carolina: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in Raleigh: a checklist

  1. Look the roofing contractor up in the official North Carolina license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Raleigh Planning and Development and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our roofing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Roofing licensing in North Carolina

North Carolina has no separate roofing board. Roofing is licensed through the NCLBGC as the S(Roofing) specialty classification, which is also part of the Building and Residential contractor classifications.

Building permits in Raleigh

Residential building permits for Raleigh are issued by City of Raleigh Planning and Development. Permits are commonly required for re-roofing and structural roof repairs. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for North Carolina

North Carolina roofs see hail, wind, heat and freeze-thaw cycles. After a storm, get an independent inspection before signing anything with a contractor who knocked on your door.
