🏠 Roofing in Tacoma, WA

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for roofing work in Tacoma? Start with the facts that are specific to Tacoma and Washington: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in Tacoma: a checklist

  1. Look the roofing contractor up in the official Washington license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for proof of the insurance or bond Washington requires (listed below) and current workers' compensation coverage.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Tacoma Planning and Development Services and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our roofing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Roofing licensing in Washington

Washington requires all construction contractors to register with L&I; a roofer can register as a specialty contractor, which may only do work in its registered specialty, or as a general contractor.

Who licenses itWashington State Department of Labor & Industries (L&I)
Insurance / bondSpecialty contractors: $15,000 surety bond; general contractors: $30,000 surety bond. General liability insurance of $200,000 public liability and $50,000 property damage, or $250,000 combined single limit.

Building permits in Tacoma

Residential building permits for Tacoma are issued by City of Tacoma Planning and Development Services. Permits are commonly required for re-roofing and structural roof repairs. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Washington

Rain, moss and algae are common roof issues in parts of Washington; inland areas add heat stress. Ask about moss-resistant materials and proper flashing and drainage.
