🔧 Plumbing in Providence, RI

License checks, permits, local firms and pay · Sources checked November 2026

Hiring for plumbing work in Providence? Start with the facts that are specific to Providence and Rhode Island: who licenses the work, how to check a license, where permits come from, and what the local market looks like. Every figure below links to its official source.

Before you hire in Providence: a checklist

  1. Look the plumber up in the official Rhode Island license lookup and make sure the name and license number match the contract.
  2. Ask for a certificate of liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage before work starts.
  3. Confirm who will pull the permit with City of Providence Department of Inspection and Standards and that final inspection is included in the price.
  4. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope; our plumbing guide covers typical costs, questions to ask and red flags.

Plumbing licensing in Rhode Island

Rhode Island licenses plumbers through the Department of Labor and Training's Division of Professional Regulation. Licenses can be checked with the division's online trade license search.

Building permits in Providence

Residential building permits for Providence are issued by City of Providence Department of Inspection and Standards. Permits are commonly required for water heaters, gas lines and repiping. Ask your contractor who will pull the permit and book the inspections; a contractor who asks you to pull it yourself is a warning sign.

Seasonal notes for Rhode Island

Frozen and burst pipes are the signature plumbing risk in Rhode Island. Insulate exposed lines, disconnect hoses before winter and keep your main shutoff easy to reach.
