Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a licensed contractor in Grand Prairie?
Always verify a contractor's license with TX's licensing board before hiring. Ask for proof of insurance, check reviews on Google and Houzz, and get at least three written bids. Licensed contractors in Grand Prairie are required to pull permits, protecting you legally.
How much does a home addition cost in Grand Prairie?
Home additions in Grand Prairie average $150–$300 per square foot depending on complexity, materials, and finishes. A 400 sq ft addition could run $60,000–$120,000. Always get a detailed written estimate that breaks out labor, materials, and permits.
What permits are required for remodeling in Grand Prairie?
Most structural changes, electrical work, plumbing, HVAC modifications, and additions require permits in Grand Prairie. Your licensed contractor should handle all permit applications. Unpermitted work can cause issues when you sell your home.
How long does a kitchen remodel take?
A mid-sized kitchen remodel typically takes 4–8 weeks for an experienced Grand Prairie contractor. Complex projects with custom cabinetry and structural changes can run 10–16 weeks. Ask for a detailed project timeline in your written contract.
What should a contractor contract include?
A solid contract should include: project scope and specifications, start and completion dates, payment schedule (never pay more than 10% upfront), materials list, change-order process, warranty terms, and proof of insurance. Your Grand Prairie contractor should provide all of this in writing.
