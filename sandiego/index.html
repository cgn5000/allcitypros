Building permits in San Diego

Residential building permits are issued by City of San Diego Development Services Department. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

San Diego climate at a glance

San Diego averages 1,320 heating degree days and 1,019 cooling degree days a year, with 11.0 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.

Source: NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals 1991-2020, station USW00003131. Checked November 15, 2026.
