Building permits in Anchorage

Residential building permits are issued by Municipality of Anchorage Development Services. Your contractor should normally pull the permit and schedule inspections.

Anchorage climate at a glance

Anchorage averages 9,979 heating degree days and 8 cooling degree days a year, with 16.4 inches of precipitation. That shapes HVAC sizing and roofing choices; see the HVAC and roofing pages for what it means.

Source: NOAA NCEI U.S. Climate Normals 1991-2020, station USW00026451. Checked November 15, 2026.
